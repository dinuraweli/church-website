The Seven Sacraments

Encountering Christ through the sacred mysteries of the Church

The sacraments are outward signs instituted by Christ to give grace. Through them, we encounter Jesus and receive God's blessing in our lives. The Catholic Church celebrates seven sacraments, which are divided into three categories: Sacraments of Initiation, Sacraments of Healing, and Sacraments of Service.

Baptism

Baptism is the first sacrament of initiation and the gateway to all other sacraments. Through baptism, we become children of God, members of the Church, and are cleansed from original sin.

At St. Mary's Church:

For Infants and Children:

  • Parents must be registered parishioners or obtain permission from their home parish
  • Parents are required to attend a pre-baptism preparation class
  • At least one godparent must be a confirmed Catholic in good standing

Required Documents:

  • Birth certificate of the child
  • Marriage certificate of parents (if applicable)
  • Baptismal certificate of godparents
  • Letter of good standing from godparents' parish

Schedule: Baptisms are typically celebrated on the first and third Sundays of each month after the 9:30 AM Mass. Please contact the church office at least one month in advance to schedule.

Confirmation

Confirmation completes baptismal grace. Through this sacrament, we receive the Holy Spirit more fully, are strengthened in faith, and become more perfectly bound to the Church.

At St. Mary's Church:

Preparation Program:

  • Candidates must be baptized Catholics
  • Typically administered to youth in Grade 10 or older
  • Two-year preparation program required
  • Regular attendance at Sunday Mass is expected
  • Participation in retreat and service projects

Required Documents:

  • Baptismal certificate
  • Letter from sponsor (must be a confirmed Catholic)
  • Completion of preparation classes

Schedule: Confirmation is typically celebrated once a year by the Bishop. Registration for preparation begins in August. Contact the church office for more details.

Holy Communion (Eucharist)

The Eucharist is the source and summit of Christian life. In this sacrament, we receive the Body and Blood of Christ, truly present under the appearances of bread and wine.

At St. Mary's Church:

First Holy Communion Preparation:

  • Children typically receive First Communion in Grade 2 or later
  • Must be baptized and attending Sunday School or catechism classes
  • Two-year preparation program including instruction in the faith
  • Must have received the Sacrament of Reconciliation first
  • Parents are expected to attend preparation sessions

Required Documents:

  • Baptismal certificate
  • Regular attendance at Sunday School or catechism classes
  • Parent consent form

Schedule: First Holy Communion is typically celebrated in May. Registration begins in September of the previous year.

Reconciliation (Confession)

Also known as Penance or Confession, this sacrament offers God's forgiveness for sins committed after Baptism. Through this sacrament, we are reconciled with God and the Church.

At St. Mary's Church:

Confession Times:

  • Every Saturday: 4:00 PM - 5:00 PM
  • Before major feast days (as announced)
  • By appointment (contact the parish office)

First Reconciliation:

  • Children receive First Reconciliation before First Communion
  • Preparation included in Sunday School program
  • Parents participate in preparation sessions

Process: Enter the confessional, make the sign of the cross, confess your sins to the priest, receive absolution, and complete your penance.

Anointing of the Sick

This sacrament provides spiritual healing and strength to those who are seriously ill, elderly, or preparing for surgery. It can be received multiple times during illness.

At St. Mary's Church:

Who Should Receive:

  • Anyone seriously ill or weakened by old age
  • Those preparing for major surgery
  • Anyone in danger of death from illness or accident
  • Can be received multiple times if condition worsens

How to Arrange:

  • Contact the parish office immediately when needed
  • Priests are available 24/7 for emergencies
  • Communal Anointing of the Sick celebrated during special Masses
  • Home visits can be arranged for those unable to come to church

Important: Please don't wait until the last moment to call for a priest. The sacrament is meant for healing and should be received when someone first becomes seriously ill.

Holy Orders

Through Holy Orders, men are ordained to serve the Church as deacons, priests, or bishops. This sacrament imparts a sacred power and responsibility to shepherd God's people.

Discerning a Vocation:

Requirements:

  • Must be a baptized and confirmed Catholic male
  • Deep prayer life and commitment to the Church
  • Years of seminary formation and preparation
  • Evaluation and recommendation by the diocese

For Those Discerning:

  • Speak with the parish priest about your calling
  • Contact the diocesan Vocations Director
  • Participate in vocations retreats and programs
  • Pray for guidance and seek spiritual direction

Support: Our parish actively supports vocations to the priesthood and religious life. We encourage young men to consider this sacred calling and offer guidance in discernment.

Matrimony (Marriage)

The Sacrament of Matrimony unites a man and woman in a lifelong covenant of love, mirroring Christ's love for the Church. Marriage is a sacred bond blessed by God.

At St. Mary's Church:

Preparation Requirements:

  • At least one partner must be a baptized Catholic
  • Contact the parish office at least 6 months before intended wedding date
  • Attend Pre-Cana (marriage preparation) program
  • Meet with the priest for pre-marriage counseling sessions
  • Both partners must be free to marry (not previously married or prior marriage annulled)

Required Documents:

  • Baptismal certificates (issued within 6 months of wedding)
  • Confirmation certificates
  • Letter of freedom to marry from home parish (if from another parish)
  • Civil marriage license
  • Completed pre-marriage questionnaires
  • Proof of completion of marriage preparation program

Planning: Wedding ceremonies can be scheduled on Saturdays and other approved days. Discuss specific dates and times with the parish priest.

Need More Information?

If you have questions about any of the sacraments or would like to schedule a sacrament, please contact our parish office.
