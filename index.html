Welcome to Our Parish

St. Mary's Church Dehiwala has been a cornerstone of faith in our community for many years. We are a vibrant Catholic parish dedicated to worship, fellowship, and service. Whether you are a long-time parishioner or visiting for the first time, we welcome you with open arms.

Our church family is committed to growing in faith together, supporting one another, and reaching out to those in need. We invite you to join us in worship, participate in our ministries, and become part of our loving community.
