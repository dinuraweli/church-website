Parish Associations

Get to know more about the associations in our church.

Our parish is blessed with many active associations and groups that serve our community and help parishioners grow in faith. Whether you're looking to serve others, deepen your spiritual life, or connect with fellow parishioners, there's a place for you!

Legion of Mary

The Legion of Mary is a lay apostolic association dedicated to service and evangelization under the guidance of the Holy Spirit and the Blessed Virgin Mary.

Activities:

  • Home visitations and prayer
  • Visiting the sick and elderly
  • Street evangelization
  • Supporting parish activities
  • Rosary recitation and devotions

Meetings: Every Tuesday at 6:00 PM

Open to: All parishioners committed to serving Mary and the Church

St. Vincent de Paul Society

The Society of St. Vincent de Paul is dedicated to serving the poor, the suffering, and the marginalized, following the example of our patron saint.

Activities:

  • Assisting families in need
  • Food distribution programs
  • Financial assistance for education and medical needs
  • Home visits to the poor and sick
  • Clothing and essentials distribution

Meetings: First Sunday of each month after 8:00 AM Mass

Open to: All who wish to serve Christ in the poor

YCS

The Catholic Women's League brings together women of faith for fellowship, spiritual growth, and service to the parish and community.

Activities:

  • Monthly spiritual gatherings
  • Organizing parish social events
  • Supporting church maintenance and decoration
  • Fundraising for charitable causes
  • Prayer groups and retreats

Meetings: Second Saturday of each month at 3:00 PM

Open to: All women of the parish

Youth Association

Our Youth Ministry provides a vibrant community for young people to grow in faith, form lasting friendships, and discover their calling in Christ.

Activities:

  • Weekly youth meetings and Bible study
  • Sports and recreational activities
  • Service projects and community outreach
  • Annual youth camp and retreats
  • Music ministry and praise sessions

Meetings: Every Friday at 5:30 PM

Open to: Youth ages 13-25

Altar Servers Society

Altar servers assist the priest during Mass and other liturgical celebrations, serving with reverence and dedication.

Activities:

  • Serving at daily and Sunday Masses
  • Assisting at special celebrations and feast days
  • Regular training and formation sessions
  • Annual altar servers' retreat
  • Participation in diocesan events

Training: First Saturday of each month at 9:00 AM

Open to: Boys and girls aged 10 and above who have received First Communion

Choir

Our choirs enhance our liturgical celebrations through sacred music, leading the congregation in worship and praise.

Groups:

  • Main Choir (English)
  • Sinhala Choir
  • Youth Choir
  • Children's Choir
  • Instrumentalists group

Practice: Varies by group; typically Saturday evenings

Open to: Anyone with a love for music and worship

Holy Childhood

Dedicated catechists who teach the faith to children and prepare them for the sacraments.

Responsibilities:

  • Teaching Sunday School classes
  • Preparing children for First Communion and Confirmation
  • Organizing children's activities and events
  • Ongoing formation and training

Meetings: Monthly planning meetings

Requirements: Commitment to faith formation and love for children

Mothers Union

Ministers bring Holy Communion to the sick and homebound, providing spiritual comfort and companionship.

Activities:

  • Taking Holy Communion to the sick and elderly
  • Prayer with those in hospitals and nursing homes
  • Coordinating priest visits for sacraments
  • Providing companionship and support

Training: Provided by the parish

Open to: Extraordinary Ministers of Holy Communion and compassionate parishioners

Human Development

A ministry dedicated to strengthening marriages and helping couples grow closer to each other and to God.

Activities:

  • Weekend retreats for married couples
  • Monthly enrichment sessions
  • Support for engaged couples
  • Marriage renewal celebrations

Meetings: Third Sunday of each month

Open to: Married couples and those preparing for marriage

Liturgy Committee

Trained lay ministers who assist in distributing Holy Communion during Mass and to the sick.

Responsibilities:

  • Distributing Holy Communion at Mass
  • Bringing Communion to the homebound
  • Assisting at special liturgies

Training: Comprehensive training and commissioning by the Archbishop

Requirements: Active Catholic in good standing, approved by the parish priest

Parish Pastoral Council

Lectors proclaim the Word of God during Mass, helping the assembly to hear and understand the Scriptures.

Responsibilities:

  • Reading the first and second readings at Mass
  • Leading the Prayer of the Faithful
  • Preparing and practicing readings

Training: Initial training and ongoing formation sessions

Open to: Confirmed Catholics with clear speaking ability

Family Lofe Committee

The first face of our parish community, welcoming parishioners and visitors with warmth and hospitality.

Responsibilities:

  • Greeting people as they arrive
  • Distributing bulletins and materials
  • Assisting with seating during crowded Masses
  • Taking up the collection
  • Ensuring safety and order

Schedule: Sign up for specific Mass times

Open to: All welcoming and friendly parishioners

Ready to Get Involved?

We invite you to join one or more of our parish associations. Your time, talents, and presence are valued and needed!

For more information about any of these groups, please contact the parish office or speak with the coordinator after Mass.
