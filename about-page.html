About St. Mary's Church, Dehiwala

About St. Mary's Church Dehiwala

St. Mary's Church Dehiwala is a vibrant Catholic parish dedicated to growing in faith, fostering fellowship, and serving others with love and compassion. We are a diverse community of believers united in our devotion to Jesus Christ and the Blessed Virgin Mary.

Parish Leadership

Our parish is blessed with dedicated clergy and lay leaders who work tirelessly to serve our community. The Parish Priest, along with assistant priest and a team of committed volunteers, guides our liturgical and pastoral activities.

Mass Schedule

We offer daily Mass throughout the week:

  • Weekday Mass: Monday to Friday at 6:00 AM
  • Weekday Mass (Evening): Tuesday to Friday at 6:00 PM
  • Saturday Mass (Evening): 7:00 AM, 6:00 PM
  • Sunday Mass (English): 6:00 AM, 7:30 AM, 6:30 PM
  • Sunday Mass (Sinhala): 10:15 AM
  • Sunday Mass (Tamil): 9:00 AM
  • Adoration: First Friday of every month
