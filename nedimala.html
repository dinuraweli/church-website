Infant Jesus Church Nedimala

Infant Jesus Church Nedimala is our beloved sub-church, serving as an important extension of St. Mary's Church Dehiwala. This chapel holds a special place in the hearts of our parishioners and continues to be a center of devotion and community worship.

About Infant Jesus Church

Infant Jesus Church Nedimala has been serving the faithful in the Nedimala area for many years. The church is known for its devotion to the Infant Jesus and provides a convenient place of worship for families in the surrounding neighborhoods.

Mass Schedule

Infant Jesus Church offers the following Mass schedule:

  • Sunday Mass: 7:00 AM (Sinhala)
  • Special Feast Days: As announced

Devotions and Activities

The chapel is a center for various devotional activities and community gatherings:

  • Novenas to the Infant Jesus
  • Rosary recitation
  • Feast day celebrations
  • Community prayer meetings
  • Catechism classes for local children

Parish Connection

While Infant Jesus Church Nedimala operates as a sub-church, it maintains close ties with St. Mary's Church Dehiwala. Parishioners from both communities often come together for major celebrations and events, strengthening the bonds of our larger parish family.

Community Outreach

The church serves as a focal point for community service in the Nedimala area, including charitable activities, support for families in need, and pastoral care for the sick and elderly.

Getting There

Infant Jesus Church is located in Nedimala, easily accessible from St. Mary's Church Dehiwala. Visitors are always welcome to join us for Mass and prayer.
