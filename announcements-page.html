Parish Announcements

Weekly Parish Announcements

Our Sunday School is a vital part of our parish's mission to nurture the faith of our youngest members. We provide a loving and engaging environment where children can learn about God's love, the teachings of Jesus, and the rich traditions of the Catholic faith.

About Our Sunday School

The Sunday School at St. Mary's Church offers faith formation for children from preschool through high school. Our dedicated team of catechists uses age-appropriate curricula and creative teaching methods to help children grow in their understanding and love of God.

Classes and Schedule

Sunday School classes are held every Sunday after the 8:00 AM Mass. Children are grouped by age and grade level to ensure appropriate instruction:

  • Preschool (Ages 4-5): Introduction to God's love through stories, songs, and activities
  • Primary (Grades 1-3): Basic prayers, Bible stories, and preparation for First Holy Communion
  • Middle (Grades 4-6): Deeper exploration of the faith, sacraments, and Christian living
  • High School (Grades 7-12): Confirmation preparation, apologetics, and faith in action

Sacramental Preparation

Our Sunday School program includes special preparation for the sacraments of First Holy Communion and Confirmation. These programs typically run for one to two years and involve both classroom instruction and family participation.

Registration

New students are welcome to join at any time during the year. Registration forms are available at the church office or after Sunday Mass. Parents are encouraged to be actively involved in their children's faith formation.

Contact Information

For more information about our Sunday School program, please contact the church office or speak with our Sunday School coordinator after Mass.
